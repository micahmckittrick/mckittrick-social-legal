Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how McKittrick Social ("the Service"), operated by McKittrick LLC ("we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Service. By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

The Service is a self-hosted social media management tool. When you connect a third-party account (such as TikTok or Instagram), we store the access tokens and basic profile information (such as your username, display name, and profile picture) provided by that platform. We collect the content you choose to create, schedule, and publish through the Service.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information solely to provide the Service's functionality: authenticating with connected platforms, scheduling content, and publishing posts to the accounts you have authorized. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

3. TikTok Data

When you connect a TikTok account, we access only the scopes you approve, which may include your basic profile information and the ability to upload and publish video content on your behalf. We use this access exclusively to perform the actions you request within the Service. We do not share your TikTok data with any third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

Access tokens and account data are stored on our own self-hosted infrastructure. Media files may be stored on secure cloud storage to enable publishing. We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain connected-account data only as long as your account remains connected. You may disconnect any account at any time, which revokes our access and removes the associated tokens. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us.

6. Third-Party Platforms

Your use of connected platforms is governed by their own privacy policies, including TikTok's Privacy Policy. We are not responsible for the practices of those platforms.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
