Privacy Policy

Bills - Simple Tracking
Last updated: July 24, 2026

Data Collection

Bills does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All bill information, payment details, and notes are stored locally on your device and optionally synced via your personal iCloud account.

iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud, your data is synced through Apple's CloudKit infrastructure. We do not have access to your iCloud data. Apple's privacy policy governs iCloud data handling.

Analytics

Bills does not include any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.

Notifications

Bill reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to external servers.
